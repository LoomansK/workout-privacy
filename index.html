Privacy Policy for Titanrep

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Titanrep does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

Your workout history, sets, reps, weights, program selections, and preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. This data never leaves your device except as described below.

Apple Health

If you choose to log a completed workout to Apple Health, Titanrep sends the workout type, duration, and timing to the Health app on your device using Apple's native HealthKit framework. This happens only when you finish a workout and only if you've granted permission. Titanrep does not read your health data.

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking

Titanrep requires no account or sign-in. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking software.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Titanrep does not see or store your payment information.

Data export

You can export your workout history as a file from within the app at any time. Exported files are created on your device for your own use.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
