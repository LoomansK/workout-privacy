About Titanrep

Titanrep is a personal strength training tracker for iPhone. It helps you follow workout programs, log sets and reps, and track your progress over time.

Subscriptions

Titanrep offers a 7-day free trial on monthly and annual subscriptions. You can also purchase a lifetime unlock with a one-time payment. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.

  • To manage or cancel your subscription, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.
  • To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
