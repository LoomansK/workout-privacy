Privacy Policy for Workout

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Overview

Workout is a personal strength training tracker. This privacy policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and where it is stored.

Data We Collect

Workout Data

  • Exercises performed, including names, muscle groups, and images
  • Sets logged, including repetitions, weight in pounds, and timestamps
  • Workout duration, notes, and subjective feeling ratings
  • Program settings, including selected training split, phase, and exercise preferences

HealthKit Data

With your permission, Workout writes completed strength training workouts to Apple Health (HealthKit), including:

  • Workout type (Traditional Strength Training)
  • Duration in minutes
  • Start and end timestamps

Workout does not read data from Apple Health.

How Data Is Used

  • Workout data is used to display your training history, track progression, and pre-fill weights and reps for future workouts.
  • HealthKit data is written solely to log your completed workouts in the Apple Health app at your request.

Data Storage

  • All workout data is stored locally on your device in an on-device SQLite database.
  • No workout data is transmitted to any server, third party, or the developer.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party. The only external integration is Apple HealthKit, which receives workout data only when you complete a workout and have granted HealthKit write permission.

Your Controls

  • You can revoke HealthKit permission at any time in the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security > Health.
  • You can delete individual workouts from the History tab.
  • Deleting the app deletes all local data.

Children's Privacy

Workout is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact
